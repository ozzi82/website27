## Findings from reading the code (coordinator, read-only)
- Glow: `GlowMaterial` (SceneMaterials.tsx) uses GLOW_INTENSITY 0.8 and a face colour of glow*0.15 at night; `NightEffects` bloom has luminanceThreshold 0.7 and ACES tone mapping. Emissive 0.8 x glow colour barely clears the bloom threshold, so face-lit (LP 5, 11-F, 11-FS, 11-N, 11-C) and side-lit (11-S, 11-BS, 11-FS) letters do not read as light sources. Needs a real glow: much higher emissive (about 2-4, tuned), lower threshold / higher bloom, plus a soft light spill (face-lit: faint wall wash around/in front; side-lit and flush letters: thin light leak on the wall around the edge, `glowParts` currently returns wallSpill "flush" only for partial-back, add it for full side / partial-front). Must still look right in day mode and at low dimmer level, and not blow colours out to white (verify with saturated red/blue glow colours, not just white).
- Profile "tube" (LP 11-N) in `useSignGeometry.ts` builds a symmetric bevel with radius `tubeRadius(depth, halfStroke)` = min(depth/2, 0.85*halfStroke) on BOTH caps -> the whole letter becomes a tube. Owner fact: the LP 11-N tool rounds the edge by at most 0.5" (12.7 mm) and never more than half the thickness; only the FRONT edge is rounded. Lighting for 11-N: face lit + the front 50% of the side wall lit (including the rounded part), the back 50% unlit/painted. Add a `bandFraction` (0..1 of depth) to the light model (e.g. `side: "partial-front"` plus `sideBand?: 0.5` on LightConfig or LightBehavior) and use it instead of the fixed `sideBandThickness` for 11-N; 11-FS keeps the nominal brochure band. Update the 11-N copy/spec text in configurations.ts so it says exactly this (front edge rounded up to 0.5", half of the side lit with the face) and keep other pages consistent (`npm run export:content`, llms.txt, tests). The conical profile (11-C) keep as is unless the same glow problem needs the same glow fixes.
- Depths: `STEEL_DEPTHS` = [30, 50, 75, 100] and LP 11 is 25-30 mm; with default depth presumably the smallest, LP 3.1/3.2/5 look the same as LP 11. Make steel (LP 3.1, 3.2, 5) clearly thicker by default: default depth 75 mm for those (check where the default depth is chosen; keep LP 11 at 30), and make sure the rendered proportion difference is obvious at the default camera. LP 1 default depth should match whichever build option is chosen (below).
- LP 1 materials (new, LP 1 only): finish choice: wood, mirror gold stainless steel, brushed stainless steel, corten, clear acrylic, clear acrylic with coloured/painted front, coloured acrylic. Plus a "build" choice: Solid material (thinner depth options, e.g. 3/5/10/20 mm) vs Fabricated (hollow, thicker options, e.g. 20/50/100/200 mm; offered only for metal finishes that can be fabricated: stainless/corten; wood and acrylic are solid-only; decide sensibly and say so in the UI copy). Implement as PBR materials: procedural textures for wood grain, brushed stainless (anisotropic-looking streaks), corten (rust noise), real reflective mirror gold (needs an environment map: check what lighting exists; add a small procedural/`drei` Environment preset that works offline-safe and does not break the night/day mood), clear acrylic via transmission/refraction-lite or tinted transparency (keep performance okay on software WebGL fallback and low-end phones; degrade gracefully), coloured acrylic, clear with painted/coloured front. Wire into ConfiguratorState, ConfigControls (compact, no vertical scroll growth: the owner does not want to scroll for options), quote card summary, deep link params, quote hand-off to HubSpot (configuration description string), configSummary and unit tests. Do not change the brochure facts of LP 1 except to add the materials info clearly labelled as available finishes/builds as stated by the owner.
