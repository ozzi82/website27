# Owner clarification: product taxonomy (2026-10-03, authoritative)

This OVERRIDES any conflicting statement in `2026-10-03-wholesale-repositioning-brief.md` and in the copy/data built from it.

## Product lines (what Sunlite actually sells)
- **Ultra-slim letters = EdgeLuxe LP 11 series = cast block acrylic letters** (25-30 mm; the whole LP 11 family:
  LP 11-F face-lit, 11-B back/halo-lit, 11-FB face+halo, 11-BS partial back side-lit, 11-FS face + partial front side-lit,
  11-S full side-lit, 11-N faux neon, 11-C conical). This is Sunlite's **signature product and the main focus** of the site.
  "Ultra-slim" and "cast block acrylic" are the same product line (do not present them as two products).
- **Classic letters = trimless letters = EdgeLuxe LP 5, LP 3.1, LP 3.2** (fabricated stainless steel; LP 5 face-lit trimless,
  LP 3.1 halo on standoffs, LP 3.2 flush-mount partial side-lit halo). These are Sunlite's "classic" channel letters.
- **LP 1 = everything non-illuminated** (flat cutout letters).
- **Custom sign fabrication** = custom work; here we can add **blade signs** and **push-through cabinet signs** (these two are
  allowed ONLY as part of custom sign fabrication, not as standalone core products).
- **We do NOT offer trim-capped letters.** There is no "Trimmed" option. The existing trim-cap illustration/comparison image may be
  kept, but only as a COMPARISON: it shows what a conventional trim-cap channel letter looks like versus what Sunlite builds and why Sunlite's
  construction is better. Never list "trimmed" as something Sunlite offers.

## Naming of lighting codes (brochure letters)
F = face, B = back (halo), S = side, N = neon, C = conical. So **LP 11-FS = face lit AND partially (front) side lit** (face glows;
a thin band of light also glows at the front edge of the side wall).

## Configurator
The 3D configurator (`/configurator`, "Build Your Sign") is a key differentiator: no other company offers this. It should be
**highly promoted** across the site.
